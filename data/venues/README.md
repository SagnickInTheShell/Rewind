# Venue format

A venue is a JSON file matching `rewind.schemas.venue.Venue` (see `docs/DATA_CONTRACTS.md`). All coordinates are **metres** in a flat venue frame. For the demo venue, x points east, y points south, and the origin is the north-west corner.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `venue_id`, `name` | Identifier and display name |
| `bounds` | `[min, max]` corner points of the floor plan |
| `zones[]` | Analysis cells. `polygon` is a list of `{x, y}` points (≥ 3). `kind` is one of `floor`, `corridor`, `entry`, `exit`, `gate_area` |
| `portals[]` | Openings between two zones, or between a zone and `"OUTSIDE"`. `segment` is the physical opening. It must lie on the boundary of the connected zones, within 0.2 m. `width_m` sets the capacity: `width_m × specific_flow` people/s. `is_open: false` closes it; the simulator treats it as a wall. `bidirectional: false` makes it one-way, `from_zone → to_zone` |
| `walls[]` | Wall segments `{a, b}` that people cannot cross. Leave gaps in perimeter walls where gates are |
| `sources` | Zones where people enter (via portals from `OUTSIDE`) |
| `sinks` | Zones with exit portals to `OUTSIDE` |
| `calibration` | ≥ 4 pairs of `image_points` (pixels in the **original** video resolution) and matching `world_points` (metres) |

The loader checks the following and reports readable errors:

- Every zone is reachable from a source.
- Every source can reach an exit with all portals open.
- Every sink has an exit portal.
- Portal segments lie on zone boundaries.
- IDs are unique.
- There are at least 4 calibration pairs.

## Demo venue (`demo_venue.json`)

The demo venue is generated by `scripts/gen_demo_venue.py` from `rewind/venue/demo.py`.

```
 x=0        10        20        30
 y=0 ┌────────┬──GATE_A──┬────────┐   GATE_A: entry, 4 m wide, north side of A2 (source)
     │   A1   │    A2    │   A3   │
6.67 ├────────┼──────────┼────────┤
     │   B1   │    B2    │   B3   │
13.3 ├──wall┄┄┼──────────┼────────┤   partial wall B1|C1 (x 0–6 m) creates a bottleneck
     │   C1   │    C2    │   C3   ┊ GATE_C: side exit, 3.5 m, east side of C3 — CLOSED at start
  20 └────────┴──GATE_B──┴────────┘   GATE_B: main exit, 3 m, south side of C2 (overloaded sink)
```

All adjacent zones are connected by full-width corridor portals (`C_A1_A2`, `C_B2_C2`, …).

### Calibration

The shipped calibration matches the **synthetic top-down camera** used by `scripts/render_synthetic_video.py`: a 1280×720 frame at 32 px/m, with the venue's north-west corner at pixel (160, 40). **For real footage these values are placeholders.** Recalibrate with the calibration helper on the Upload page: click 4 or more ground points on the first video frame and enter their floor-plan coordinates. The helper saves them into the venue's `calibration` via `PUT /api/venues/{venue_id}`.

Tips for a good calibration:

- Use ground-level points (floor markings, the bases of pillars, gate posts), not heads or signs.
- Spread the points across the whole visible floor. With more than 4 points, RANSAC is used.
